Swarmfall · field guide

Build the ship

Everything that makes your ship stronger: all 27 boosters and all 14 station modules, with what each one really does in numbers.

Boosters

Last for one run. A shuttle brings a choice of three after waves 1, 4 and 9, then after every 5th wave (14, 19 and so on), and you get a stronger choice each time you leave the station after a boss. Passing on all three pays ◆ 10.

27 boosters

Station modules

Yours for good. A new ship has 2; each boss you destroy has the next one fitted. You raise their levels with scrap, at the station between runs or while docked.

14 modules

Boosters

Each of the three cards is drawn on its own, and the same booster never appears twice in one offer. A booster already at its top level is not offered again. If nothing of the drawn rarity is left, you get a card of another rarity.

Common

7 boosters

The everyday picks. Most cards you are offered are these.

Rapid Feed

Weapons

+20% fire rate.

CommonUp to 8 levels

Each level adds 20% of your base fire rate. A new ship fires 2.75 volleys a second; all 8 levels make that 7.2.

Heavy Rounds

Weapons

+25% damage.

CommonUp to 8 levels

Each level adds 25% of your base damage. A shot does 10; all 8 levels make that 30.

Side Thrusters

Specials

+15% steering speed.

CommonUp to 4 levels

Each level adds 15% steering speed. All 4 make the ship 60% quicker side to side.

Hull Plate

Armor

+1 max hull, so you can take 1 more hit. Repairs 1 now.

CommonUp to 2 levels

Each level adds 1 hull and repairs 1 on the spot. A ship can add 2 hull at the most, counting the station's Reinforced Hull: with that fitted, one Hull Plate is the limit; without it, two.

Rail Coils

Weapons

+25% shot speed and +10% damage.

CommonUp to 4 levels

Each level adds 25% shot speed and 10% damage. All 4 double shot speed and add 40% damage.

Tractor Field

Specials

Pull scrap in from much farther away.

CommonUp to 3 levels

Pickup reach grows from 42 to 102, 162, then 222: about five times as far at level 3.

Patch Kit

Armor

Repair 2 hull now.

CommonNo limit

Repairs 2 hull now and nothing else. Only offered while your hull is damaged. No limit on how often.

Rare

11 boosters

New weapons and systems. These are what the booster after a boss is mostly made of.

Burst Thrusters

Specials

Flick your thumb up to burst forward, or down to drop back. The ship drifts home by itself. (Keys: up and down.)

RareUp to 1 level

A quick flick of the thumb up the screen lunges the ship 110 units forward, about a seventh of the way up the field; a flick down drops it 48 back, as far as it can go and stay on the screen. It holds there a moment, then drifts home by itself, and the next burst is ready once it is home. On a keyboard: the up and down arrows, or W and S. It makes the ship no harder to hit: it only moves it.

Split Barrel

Weapons

Extra shots on some volleys, then on all: see below.

RareUp to 3 levels

Level 1: every 3rd volley fires 1 extra shot. Level 2: every volley fires 1 extra. Level 3: every volley fires 2 extra, so a one-barrel ship fires 3 shots a volley (4 with the Twin Mount module).

Piercing Core

Weapons

Shots pass through 1 more enemy.

RareUp to 3 levels

Each level lets a shot pass through 1 more enemy, up to 3. A shot bounces (Ricochet) only after its passes are used up.

Aegis Shell

Shields

A shield absorbs one hit, then recharges. More levels recharge faster.

RareUp to 3 levels

The first level gives a shield that absorbs one hit and comes back after 15 seconds. Each level after that takes 3.5 seconds off, as does owning the Aegis Generator module. Recharge never drops below 4 seconds.

Wing Drone

Specials

A drone orbits your ship and fires with you.

RareUp to 3 levels

Each level adds 1 drone, up to 3 drones counting the Drone Bay module. A drone fires every 0.7 seconds for half your shot damage.

Weak-Point Scanner

Weapons

+15% chance for a shot to deal 2.5× damage.

RareUp to 4 levels

Each level adds 15%: 60% of shots at level 4. A critical shot does 2.5× damage. Homing missiles never crit.

Volatile Payload

Weapons

A destroyed enemy has a 1 in 4 chance to explode and damage its neighbours.

RareUp to 3 levels

One destroyed enemy in four explodes. The blast damages everything within 40, 45, then 50 units, for 50%, 60%, then 75% of your shot damage. An enemy destroyed by a blast does not explode itself, so one kill never sets off a chain.

Ricochet

Weapons

After a hit, shots bounce to another enemy.

RareUp to 2 levels

Each level is 1 bounce to another enemy, up to 2 bounces per shot.

Flame Cloud

Weapons

Lob a cloud of flame ahead of your ship. Enemies that fly through it burn slowly.

RareUp to 3 levels

Every 7 seconds, then every 5.5, then every 4, the ship lobs a cloud of flame into the open lanes ahead of it, below the parked enemies. It hangs for 4.5 seconds. An enemy that flies through it burns while inside and for 3 seconds after, trailing flame and losing 6% of its full health a second (9% at level 2, 12% at level 3). Burning never takes an enemy below a tenth of its health: it weakens, your shots finish. Bosses burn at an eighth of that rate.

Scavenger

Specials

+50% scrap for the rest of this run.

RareUp to 2 levels

Each level adds 50%: double scrap at level 2. It multiplies with the Salvage Rig module.

Sentry Satellite

Specials

Deploy a satellite that fires at the swarm. It can be shot down, and it runs out after a while.

RareUp to 3 levels

The ship sets a satellite down 150 units ahead of it. It fires at the nearest enemy about twice a second (1.8, 2.2, then 2.6 shots a second) for 60% of your shot damage. It lasts 9, 11, then 13 seconds, takes 2, 3, then 4 enemy shots (a missile counts as two, and an enemy flying into it destroys it), and stops the shots that hit it. Nine seconds after it is gone, the next one deploys.

Prototype

9 boosters

The strongest and strangest. Rare early, a little more likely each wave.

Laser Drone

Specials

A drone at your wing locks a thin laser onto an enemy or a missile in flight and burns it up.

PrototypeUp to 3 levels

A drone flies off your right wing. It only takes enemies in flight: one that has left the formation, or anything a boss lets loose. It takes missiles too, and burns one out of the air in about a third of a second. It never takes a boss, nor an enemy sitting in its place, and never a latcher that has clamped onto your ship. It picks the nearest and holds a thin yellow beam on it until that enemy is destroyed or goes back to its place, then rests for two seconds and moves to the next. The beam sets the enemy alight, and the fire does the damage: 0.9, 1.3, then 1.7 times your shot damage every second, and for 2 seconds more if the beam loses it.

Seeker Pod

Specials

Launch homing missiles that deal 2.5× damage in a small blast.

PrototypeUp to 3 levels

One missile every 1.7 seconds, then every 0.85, then every 0.57. Each does 2.5× your shot damage to its target and half of that to enemies close by.

Arc Caster

Weapons

Lightning strikes the nearest enemy close to your ship, then jumps on through the ones beside it. Short reach. More levels: more enemies, further, faster.

PrototypeUp to 3 levels

About every 2 seconds, if an enemy is within 220 units of the ship (about a third of the way up the field), lightning strikes the nearest and jumps to the nearest to that, and on: up to 3 enemies, each within 95 units of the last, for 2.5× your shot damage each, so a mite or a wasp goes in one. Level 2: 4 enemies, 240 units, 3× damage, a little quicker. Level 3: 5 enemies, 260 units, 3.5×, every 1.6 seconds. While nothing is in reach it sparks at the nose of the ship. On the first wave or two little comes close enough for it; from about wave 5 it fires every few seconds. The formation is never in reach: it works on what comes down at you. It will not strike a latcher that has clamped onto the ship.

EMP Bomb

Specials

Lob a bomb ahead of your ship. Its blast ring knocks out every enemy it reaches: they cannot fire or steer, and drift the way they were going.

PrototypeUp to 3 levels

Every 9 seconds the ship lobs a bomb to wherever its ring will catch the most enemies, counting one in flight as three sitting in the formation. It bursts, and a ring spreads 100 units from it. Every enemy the ring reaches is knocked out for 3 seconds: it cannot fire or steer and drifts the way it was going, and one sitting in the formation cannot fire or leave to dive. Missiles it reaches fly straight on. Level 2: every 8 seconds, 120 units, 4 seconds. Level 3: every 7 seconds, 140 units, 5 seconds. A knocked-out enemy still hurts if it drifts into you. Bosses are not affected.

Arc Coil

Specials

Hits have a 20% chance to arc lightning to 2 nearby enemies.

PrototypeUp to 3 levels

Each level adds 20%: 60% of hits at level 3. The lightning jumps to up to 2 enemies near the one you hit, for 70% of the shot's damage each.

Overdrive

Weapons

+40% fire rate and +20% damage, but −1 max hull.

PrototypeUp to 2 levels

Each level: +40% fire rate, +20% damage, and 1 less hull. Only offered while you have more than 1 hull.

Time Dilation

Shields

Enemy shots travel 20% slower.

PrototypeUp to 2 levels

Each level slows enemy shots by 20%: 40% slower at level 2.

Confusion Gas

Specials

Lob a gas cloud ahead of your ship. An enemy that flies through it turns and rams one of its own.

PrototypeUp to 3 levels

Every 7 seconds, then every 5.5, then every 4, the ship lobs a gas cloud into the open lanes ahead of it, below the parked enemies. It hangs for 4.5 seconds. An enemy that flies through it is confused for 5 seconds: it gets no new weapon, but breaks off, chases the enemy furthest from it and rams it. The rammer is destroyed and its target loses half its health (3% for a boss). If the 5 seconds run out first it flies back to its place. Bosses and lancers cannot be confused.

Reactive Nova

Shields

Taking a hit wipes every enemy shot and blasts the whole swarm.

PrototypeUp to 2 levels

When your hull takes a hit, every enemy shot on screen is wiped and every enemy takes 45 damage per level. A mite has 10 health on wave 1.
